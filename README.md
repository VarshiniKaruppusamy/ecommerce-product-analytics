# 📊 Product Analytics for an E-commerce Platform

An end-to-end Product Analytics project using a real-world Brazilian e-commerce dataset (Olist) to analyze customer behavior, product performance, delivery operations, and sales trends.

The project demonstrates how data can be transformed into actionable business insights using Python, Power BI, and Tableau.

---

## Project Overview

This project analyzes over 100,000 e-commerce orders to answer key business questions:

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which customers contribute the most value?
- How does delivery performance impact customer satisfaction?
- What payment methods are most popular?
- What business actions can improve customer retention and profitability?

---

## Business Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Measure product performance
- Evaluate delivery efficiency
- Monitor revenue trends
- Identify opportunities to improve customer satisfaction
- Provide data-driven business recommendations

- Total Orders

- Average Review Score
- Delivery Time
- Delivery Delay
- Product Category Performance

---

## Key Business Insights

 - High-revenue categories contribute a significant share of total sales.

 - Delivery delays correlate with lower customer review scores.

 - Most customers make only one purchase, highlighting opportunities to improve retention.

 - Credit cards are the most frequently used payment method.

 - Revenue follows seasonal purchasing patterns.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Improve product quality in low-rated categories.
- Optimize delivery performance.
- Launch customer loyalty programs.
- Increase investment in top-performing categories.
- Simplify checkout for preferred payment methods.
